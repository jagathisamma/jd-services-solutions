# JD Services & Solutions — visual design system

## Brand foundation
- Deep navy `#071D55`: primary brand surface and navigation.
- Royal/electric blue `#075BD8`: secondary actions, links, focus and interaction.
- Orange `#FF6A00`: primary conversion actions and selected highlights.
- Yellow `#FFC400`: small emphasis accents only.
- White `#FFFFFF`: cards, text-on-dark and clear reading surfaces.
- Exact tagline: **Service Today... Smile Tomorrow...**
- Official logo: `assets/jd-logo.png`; use unchanged, preserve aspect ratio and transparency.

## Experience principles
1. Lead with the customer's device problem and a clear WhatsApp / call action.
2. Use a strong navy hero with one clear visual; follow with light, readable content surfaces.
3. Service cards use distinct category artwork and concise descriptions.
4. Explain the process and pickup-vs-onsite options without promising unconfirmed availability.
5. Avoid invented ratings, testimonials, certifications, warranty, pricing, 24/7 claims or service guarantees.
6. Keep the page fast: local SVG assets, explicit image dimensions, lazy-load below-fold artwork, no external font or animation dependencies.
7. Keep all tap targets comfortable, focus visible, reduced motion respected and no horizontal overflow.
8. The fixed mobile Call/WhatsApp bar must never obscure page content or footer.

## Asset policy
`assets/visual/*.svg` contains original scalable vector illustrations authored for this site. These are product-category visuals, not photographs of JD employees, real customer jobs or a physical JD workshop. Replace with approved genuine/generated photography when available; do not use full-page mockup screenshots containing fabricated copy, phone numbers, ratings, testimonials, guarantees or offers.
