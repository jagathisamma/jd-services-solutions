Good to know

Questions before you book?

A few quick answers. If your question is specific to a device or model, message us directly.

Which brands do you service?

We provide multi-brand repair support. Share your device brand and model so we can confirm whether the required service is available.

Do you provide home service?

Onsite service coordination may be available for larger appliances such as LED TVs and washing machines, depending on location, fault and technician availability. Please confirm with us first.

Can you pick up my mobile or laptop?

Pickup and return can be discussed for eligible small devices, subject to your location and current availability.

Can you arrange spare parts?

We can help source spare parts. Availability, compatibility, cost and delivery time must be confirmed for the specific device and part.

How much will the repair cost?

Costs depend on the device, fault, diagnosis and parts required. We’ll discuss an estimate before proceeding with repair work.

What details should I send on WhatsApp?

Send the device category, brand, model, your area and a short description or photo/video of the issue. Avoid sharing passwords or sensitive personal information.
